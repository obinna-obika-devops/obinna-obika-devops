Engineering approach

Operational discipline built into delivery

I prefer systems where the safe path is also the easy path: changes are versioned, validated early, observable after release and reversible when needed.

01

Automate repeatable work. Reduce operational toil and remove avoidable manual steps.

02

Treat infrastructure as software. Version, validate and review infrastructure changes.

03

Design for failure. Use health checks, SLOs, fault testing and documented recovery paths.

04

Build security into delivery. Enforce identity, policy and artifact integrity before runtime.

05

Keep changes observable and reversible. Make failures easier to detect, understand and recover from.
